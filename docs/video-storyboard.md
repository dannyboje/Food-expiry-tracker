# Fresh Ahead — How-To Video Storyboard

**Target length:** 90–120 seconds  
**Recording:** iOS Screen Recording (Control Centre) or Android built-in recorder  
**Demo data:** Tap Settings → 🎬 Load Demo Data before recording

---

## Before you record

1. Run the app on a simulator or device (`npx expo run:ios`)
2. Go to **Household** → scroll down → tap **Load Demo Data** → confirm
3. Navigate away and back to refresh all screens
4. Enable Do Not Disturb / Airplane Mode to hide notification banners
5. Set simulator to iPhone 15 Pro (6.1″) for clean screenshots

---

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Hook (0–5 s)
**Screen:** Pantry tab, freshly loaded with demo data  
**Action:** Scroll slowly down the list to show the mix of green / orange / red items  
**Narration:** *"Always know what's in your kitchen — and what needs to be used first."*

---

### Shot 2 — Add by scanning (5–18 s)
**Screen:** Tap the big ＋ button in the centre tab bar → scanner opens  
**Action:**
1. Point camera at any product barcode (cereal box works well)
2. Wait for the scan result sheet to slide up — show name, Nutri-Score badge, health score
3. Show the healthier alternatives section briefly
4. Tap "Save to Pantry"  

**Narration:** *"Scan a barcode and Fresh Ahead fills in the name, nutrition score, and even suggests healthier alternatives — instantly."*

---

### Shot 3 — Expiry date OCR (18–28 s)
**Screen:** Add Item form → tap the camera icon next to the Expiry Date field  
**Action:**
1. Point camera at a "Best Before" date on any packaging
2. The date field fills automatically  

**Narration:** *"No typing needed — just point your camera at the date and the app reads it for you."*

---

### Shot 4 — Pantry overview (28–38 s)
**Screen:** Pantry tab  
**Action:**
1. Show the red "Expired" badge on Fresh Orange Juice
2. Show the orange "Expiring soon" badge on Baby Spinach and Chicken Breast
3. Tap an item (e.g. Pringles) to open the detail view
4. Scroll down to show the health score card and alternatives  

**Narration:** *"Items are sorted by urgency. Tap any item for the full nutrition breakdown and healthier swap suggestions."*

---

### Shot 5 — Health tab & scan history (38–50 s)
**Screen:** Health tab  
**Action:**
1. Show the A–E grade breakdown chart at the top
2. Scroll down to Recent Scans — show the 5 demo products with their score chips
3. Tap "Pringles Original" → scan detail screen opens
4. Show the health score (22/100, red), alternatives cards, nutrition panel with emoji labels, additive groups  

**Narration:** *"The Health tab grades everything in your pantry and keeps a history of every scan — tap any item to see the full picture."*

---

### Shot 6 — Smart alerts (50–58 s)
**Screen:** Household tab — scroll down to the Alert Schedule section  
**Action:** Show the four tier steppers (Under 5 days, 5–14 days, 15–29 days, 30+ days)  
**Narration:** *"Smart notifications remind you before things expire — and you control exactly how much notice you get."*

---

### Shot 7 — Shopping list (58–75 s)
**Screen:** Shopping tab  
**Action:**
1. Show the Favourites section at top (Organic Whole Milk, Free Range Eggs, Greek Yogurt)
2. Tap ＋ on a favourite → list picker appears → select "Weekly Shop"
3. Show the "Weekly Shop" list with the health score chip on the pantry-sourced Pringles item
4. Tap the +/− button on "Meal Prep" list to collapse it
5. Check off "Bananas" with a tap  

**Narration:** *"Your shopping list updates automatically when items run out. Save favourites, create multiple lists, and see the health score on everything you're restocking."*

---

### Shot 8 — Recipes (75–83 s)
**Screen:** Recipes tab  
**Action:**
1. Tap several expiring items (e.g. Chicken Breast, Baby Spinach, Greek Yogurt) — checkmarks appear
2. Tap **⚠️ Use expiring** quick-select to show automatic selection
3. Choose **Indian** cuisine chip
4. Tap **Dinner** meal type card
5. Tap **✨ Generate Recipe** — show the spinner then the recipe card sliding into view
6. Briefly scroll the recipe card to show the ingredient list and numbered method steps  

**Narration:** *"Pick ingredients from your pantry, choose a cuisine, and Fresh Ahead generates a personalised recipe — perfect for using things up before they expire."*

---

### Shot 9 — Household (93–103 s)
**Screen:** Household tab  
**Action:**
1. Show "The Garcia Family" header with Alex and Sam's avatars
2. Scroll down to the food waste tracker — show Used vs Wasted this month  

**Narration:** *"Track everyone in your household and see how much food you're saving — or wasting — each month."*

---

### Shot 10 — Privacy & close (103–115 s)
**Screen:** Pantry tab (overview)  
**Action:** Hold on the pantry list — no action needed, just a clean wide shot  
**Narration:** *"Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking — ever. Download Fresh Ahead free on the App Store and Google Play."*

## Recording tips

- **iOS Simulator:** Simulator → File → Record Screen (saves .mp4 to Desktop)
- **Physical iPhone:** Add Screen Recording to Control Centre → tap to start/stop
- **Android Emulator:** Extended Controls → Screen Record
- **Trim & captions:** iMovie (free, Mac) or CapCut (free, mobile) — add the narration as captions for accessibility
- **Export:** 1080×1920 portrait for App Store previews; 1080×1350 for Instagram Reels
