🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026  ·  Fresh Ahead by Yoganjali Consultancy Services Ltd

  • We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data
  • All your data stays on your device — always
  • No account or sign-up required
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads
  • Barcode scans send only the barcode number to food databases
  • Recall data is fetched from public government APIs and matched locally

1. Who we are

Fresh Ahead is developed by Yoganjali Consultancy Services Ltd. For privacy questions contact: contact_us@yoganjaliconsultancy.co.uk

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores all data locally on your device only. We do not operate any servers that receive your data. This includes: pantry items, household profile, photos, shopping list, consumption history, and notification preferences.

3. External network calls

Barcode lookups — when you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) and USDA FoodData Central to retrieve product information. No personal data is transmitted.

Food recall checks — the App fetches public recall lists from the government agency relevant to your country. The API called depends on your device locale: US FDA (api.fda.gov/food/enforcement.json) and USDA FSIS (www.fsis.usda.gov/fsis/api/recall) for US users; UK Food Standards Agency (data.food.gov.uk/food-alerts/id.json) for UK users; FSSAI RSS feed for Indian users. The appropriate source is determined using native OS locale APIs (iOS: AppleLocale; Android: localeIdentifier), with an Intl fallback — no location permission is requested. Pantry matching and all filtering happen entirely on your device; nothing from your pantry is ever sent externally.

Healthier alternatives — when a scanned product has a low health score, the App queries Open Food Facts for better-rated products in the same category. Only category tags (e.g. "en:chocolates") and your approximate country (derived on-device from GPS) are sent — no personal data or pantry contents leave your device. If you tap the cart icon, your browser opens a Google Shopping search; that interaction is subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Voice input — when you tap the microphone button on the product name field, a short audio clip is recorded and sent to Groq (groq.com) for transcription using the Whisper model. The transcribed text is returned to the App and placed in the name field. Groq does not store audio beyond the duration of the request. This feature is entirely optional; you can add item names by typing instead. Groq's own privacy policy applies to this interaction.

4. Permissions

  • Camera — to scan barcodes and photograph expiry dates
  • Microphone — to record your voice when using the optional voice input feature on the product name field
  • Photo Library — to select existing photos of expiry dates
  • Notifications — to send local reminders before items expire (no push server involved)
  • Location (approximate) — used only to find healthier product alternatives available in your country; never stored or transmitted beyond the single category-lookup request

5. On-device ML

The App uses Google ML Kit for text recognition to read expiry dates from photos. All processing happens on your device — no photos are sent to external servers.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Your rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control at all times. You can delete individual items within the App or remove all data by uninstalling the App.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
